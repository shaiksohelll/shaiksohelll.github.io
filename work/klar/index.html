Architecture / 02

From feed noise
to usable signal.

Two source adapters enter separately. The product only speaks once the data has been normalized, deduplicated, and mapped to a stable vocabulary.

Adzuna + JSearch → shared shape → count-time company/title/city dedupe → curated taxonomy → descriptive views.

Normalization is the hinge. Both providers are mapped to a common job shape with role, skills, remote status, location, posting date, and salary-disclosure fields. A curated taxonomy resolves common aliases into canonical labels; role titles settle into practical buckets.

Dedupe is deliberately count-time. Source documents stay separate, while aggregate views collapse twins through normalized company, title, and city. Blank-company postings remain standalone rather than being guessed together.

Failure modes / 04

A partial feed
stays partial.

The safest pipeline is not the one that never fails. It is the one that refuses to turn a partial view into destructive certainty.

Freshness / 05

The status is
part of the product.

Public dataset status exposedtimestamped / source-aware

Freshness is not completeness. At the 26 August 2026 snapshot the public dataset was timestamped and JSearch had succeeded; Adzuna was partial. Klar exposes that status instead of implying a finished market.

Read-heavy aggregate views use bounded in-process caches and are invalidated after ingest. This is application-level caching, not a claim about a MongoDB cache. Salary views remain disclosure-aware: predicted or estimated pay is not presented as employer-disclosed evidence.

Boundary / 06

Public signal.
Private utility.

Public snapshot

Demand, Trends, Foresight, Atlas, Relocate, Compare, Hiring, and descriptive salary surfaces.

Authenticated utility

Watchlist, Skill Gap, and Resume Gap remain user-specific and outside this public evidence layer.

The system describes observed demand. It does not promise a salary outcome or tell a developer what they must learn. The next production step is broader source coverage—not stronger language than the evidence can support.
